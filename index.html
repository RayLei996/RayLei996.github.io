Selected work

Research, model development, and software projects

Research internship · Chinese Academy of Sciences

Particle tracking in microscopy

I worked on model training and false-positive analysis for surface plasmon resonance microscopy (SPRM), then developed and tested a standalone Windows application for analysing particle trajectories.

What I built

The desktop application brings data preprocessing, model inference, 3D trajectory visualisation, and CSV export into one workflow.

Internship · Bksuns

LLM fine-tuning with LoRA

I worked with low-rank adaptation (LoRA) to fine-tune large language models. The experience drew me toward post-training and the question of how to measure whether an adaptation helped.

Final-year project · Rhema

A system for generating maths exercises

My team developed a system that generates exercises from authored specifications. I worked on the web configuration workflow that lets users adjust an existing specification before generating a worksheet.

Course project · HMHN

Audio–visual feature fusion

In a team course project, I explored how to combine audio and video representations. We paired inputs by timestamp and used cross-modal attention to learn relationships between their features.

Design competition · Computer vision

Dynamic human reconstruction

I led video capture and collaborated on data preprocessing for a 3D human reconstruction pipeline. We adjusted the capture setup to improve the quality of the reconstruction inputs.

Career direction

I aim to become an AI algorithm engineer working on model training, post-training, optimisation, and evaluation.

Training & post-training
How should we adapt language models to a specific task and measure the resulting change in behaviour?
Reliability & efficiency
How do models hold up with limited data or across domains, and what trade-offs arise under a compute budget?
Software engineering
How can we make experiments reproducible and deliver the resulting tools in a form people can use?
